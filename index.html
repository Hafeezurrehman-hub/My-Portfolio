Pakistan Real Estate AI Lead System

A property buyer sends an enquiry at 11:23 at night. The agency is asleep. By morning that buyer has messaged three other agencies — and one of them replied first. This system is the fix.

  • Answers in under 30 seconds — at night, on Sundays, during Friday prayers.
  • Reads how people actually write. A real enquiry reads “Bahria Enclave mein 1.5 crore tak ghar chahiye, do teen mahine mein lena hai” — “a house in Bahria Enclave up to 1.5 crore (about PKR 15 million), buying within two to three months.” Area, budget, type and timeline are all in that one line, and none of it is in a format software can read. I wrote the parsers from scratch.
  • Separates buyers from browsers so agents stop losing the day to people who are only checking rates.
  • Cannot invent a price. The AI recommends only projects that exist in the agency’s own inventory — a hard guardrail, because in a property deal a made-up number is a liability.
  • Books the site visit itself into a real calendar, skipping Friday prayer hours and Sundays.
  • Follows up and reports — a decaying follow-up sequence, visit reminders, an 8 PM summary, and a live dashboard.
